Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from families considering DiGi Healthcare.

How quickly can a nurse come?
In most cases, we can have a nurse at your home within hours of your request. Scheduled care is coordinated in advance around your care plan.
How much does it cost?
Pricing varies depending on the service, frequency of visits, and duration of care. Speak to a care coordinator for a personalised quote — there's no obligation.
Are your nurses qualified?
Yes. All DiGi Health nurses are clinically certified, background-checked, and trained internally before being matched to any family. You'll know who is coming before they arrive.
What if there is an emergency?
Our nurses are trained to identify and respond to urgent situations. If escalation is needed, we coordinate directly with hospitals and family members to ensure the right support is mobilised quickly.
Do you work with hospitals?
Yes. We maintain continuity with hospital teams where needed so that post-discharge care is coordinated and nothing gets missed in the transition from facility to home.
What areas do you cover?
We currently cover Greater Nigeria including Major regions like Anambra, Delta, Abuja, and lagos. Contact us to confirm availability in your specific area.
Can I choose my nurse?
We match nurses based on your specific care needs and location to ensure the best fit. You'll always be informed of who will be visiting before they arrive.
How do I pay?
Payment options are discussed during your consultation. We accept mobile money and bank transfers. A care coordinator will walk you through everything before care begins.
